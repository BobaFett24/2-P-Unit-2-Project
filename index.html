Welcome to Chile!

(pronounced Chee-leh, not chili.)

Chile is a very thin and long country on the coast of South America

Here are some things you can see!

This is the completo; the ones in the image are the 'italiano' version, a hotdog that consists of Avocado (palta), mayonaise, and diced tomatoes. Representing the italian flag. (This is my favorite )

This is Pomaire, a traditional town est of santiago that has tons of pottery and lots of traditional foods to try.

Oh my god the bread is so good, writing this is making me hungry. The image above is the Hallula. We have 6 main types of bread but theres so many varieties and ways people make them.

Here are some animals you can see! The huemul is our national animal.

Below is an image of a really cool animal we have in our patagonia area, called the guanaco

And these are called chanchitos (little pig), three legged pig figures that are incredibly common to see in chile (especiall pomaire); they are meant to bring stability and balance.

This is Chiles breed of horse, the Chilean Corralero
